A fresh look: rooms show who hosts them, Settings is tidier, the host gets a room settings cog, and there are six more themes, light ones included.

> 🇧🇷 Resumo em português no final.

## Download

| System | File |
|---|---|
| **Windows 10/11**, most PCs | `ScreenShare-Setup-2.7.0-x64.exe` |
| **Windows 11 on ARM** (e.g. Snapdragon laptops) | `ScreenShare-Setup-2.7.0-arm64.exe` |
| **Mac with Apple Silicon** (M1 or newer), experimental | `ScreenShare-2.7.0-arm64.dmg` |
| **Mac with Intel**, experimental | `ScreenShare-2.7.0-x64.dmg` |

Works in the same rooms as 1.x and 2.x versions, so people can update when they like. Rooms show their host's picture once the host is on 2.7.0; until then, their initials. On Windows, 2.2.0 and later update themselves: look for **Restart to update** in the title bar.

## What's new

#### Added

- **Six more themes** in **Settings → Appearance**, now grouped as dark and light. Dark: **Black** (true black, for OLED screens), **Forest** and **Ember**. Light, for bright rooms: **Light**, **Paper** and **Mist**; the room's stage stays dark in them, so streams and the controls over them look as usual.

#### Changed

- **A clearer room list.** Each room shows its host's profile picture (or initials), with a red dot while someone shares and a lock when it's private, and a second line with how many people are inside, so long names fit. Rooms you've been in that don't answer are folded under **Offline**, with when you were last there, and while you're not in a room the middle shows the rooms on your network as cards to join. Pictures need the host on this version; older hosts show initials.
- **More room for the picture.** The name bar on top of each stream now hides with the controls when you leave the mouse still, the people under a focused stream are smaller and centred, and the controls sit a little higher, with more space above them.
- **A stream's right-click menu in sections**: View, Volume (a slider, with the speaker to mute) and Quality you receive, with **Stop watching** last, in red. Drawing stays under the stream, in the controls. The volume that pops up from the speaker is now a vertical slider. **Clear marks on my screen** is gone from the Sharing menu: marks fade by themselves.
- **Room settings for the host** behind a new cog next to ⓘ: Public or Private (with the PIN), **Allow drawing on streams**, **Let viewers chat** (it was the Mute chat button over the chat) and **End room for everyone**. ⓘ now shows the room's details, the same for everyone, with the address to copy.
- **Settings, tidier.** One section at a time, chosen from a list with icons; **Notifications** and **Connection** are now together under **General**. Settings sit in boxes with what each one does under its name, the themes show a small preview of the app in their colours, **Advanced** says which codec Automatic usually sends and marks **Lowest latency** as needing a restart, and **About** gathers **Copy diagnostics**, **Save to file…** and **Open logs** under **Troubleshooting**.

The installers aren't code-signed yet: on Windows click **More info → Run anyway**, on macOS open the app once, click **Done**, then **Open Anyway** in *System Settings → Privacy & Security* ([step by step](https://github.com/nsfxu/lan-screenshare/blob/main/docs/en-US/user-guide.md#opening-it-for-the-first-time-on-macos)).

Full list of changes: [CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/nsfxu/lan-screenshare/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md)

## 🇧🇷 Em português

O 2.7.0 traz um visual novo:

- **Lista de salas mais clara.** Cada sala mostra a foto de perfil de quem hospeda (ou as iniciais), um ponto vermelho enquanto alguém compartilha, um cadeado quando é privada, e quantas pessoas estão dentro. Salas que não respondem ficam recolhidas em **Offline**, com a data da última visita, e fora de uma sala o meio mostra as salas da sua rede como cartões para entrar.
- **Configurações da sala para o anfitrião**, na nova engrenagem ao lado do ⓘ: Pública ou Privada (com o PIN), **Permitir desenhar nas transmissões**, **Deixar os espectadores conversarem** e **Encerrar a sala para todo mundo**. O ⓘ agora mostra os detalhes da sala, iguais para todos.
- **Configurações mais organizadas**: uma seção por vez, com as opções em caixas e o que cada uma faz embaixo do nome; **Notificações** e **Conexão** viraram **Geral**.
- **Seis temas novos**, agora separados em escuros e claros: **Preto** (para telas OLED), **Floresta** e **Brasa**; e, para ambientes claros, **Claro**, **Papel** e **Névoa**.
- **Mais espaço para a imagem**: a barra com o nome some junto com os controles quando o mouse fica parado, e o menu do botão direito de uma transmissão ficou dividido em seções, com o volume numa barra.

Funciona nas mesmas salas que as versões 1.x e 2.x. No Windows, quem tem o 2.2.0 ou mais novo recebe a atualização sozinho (**Reinicie para atualizar**). No macOS, na primeira vez feche o aviso e clique em **Abrir Mesmo Assim** em *Ajustes do Sistema → Privacidade e Segurança* ([passo a passo](https://github.com/nsfxu/lan-screenshare/blob/main/docs/pt-BR/user-guide.md#abrir-pela-primeira-vez-no-macos)). Os instaladores ainda não são assinados: no Windows clique em **Mais informações → Executar assim mesmo**.
