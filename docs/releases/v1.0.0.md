The first release of **ScreenShare**: share your screen with the people on your network. No accounts, no cloud, just your LAN or VPN.

> 🇧🇷 Resumo em português no final.

## Download

| System | File |
|---|---|
| **Windows 10/11**, most PCs | `ScreenShare-Setup-1.0.0-x64.exe` |
| **Windows 11 on ARM** (e.g. Snapdragon laptops) | `ScreenShare-Setup-1.0.0-arm64.exe` |

| **macOS** (experimental, not tested yet) | `ScreenShare-1.0.0-universal.dmg` |

Everyone in a room needs **this same version**.

## What it does

- 🔎 **Rooms find you.** Rooms on your network appear by themselves; on a VPN or another subnet, use **Connect by IP**.
- 🔒 **Public or private rooms**, with a 4–6 digit PIN that the host can change at any time (3 wrong tries lock that computer out for 5 minutes).
- 🖥️ **Everyone can share**, several screens at once, in a grid or a spotlight. Nothing plays until you choose it, and your own stream stays hidden until you click **Show**.
- 🎚️ **Quality your way.** Up to 1080p60 (or native resolution) with hardware encoding. The streamer sets the maximum from the toolbar, each viewer picks what they receive per stream, small tiles automatically cost less, and each viewer's quality adapts to their network.
- 🔊 **Computer sound**, including 5.1/7.1 headsets, and **Leave out Discord** on Windows so the people in your call don't hear themselves.
- 💬 **Chat, profile pictures (with cropping), and host controls**: remove people, stop streams, delete messages, mute the chat.
- 🔍 **Zoom, pan and full screen**, with controls that hide when the mouse is still.
- 🔁 **Keeps working**: reconnects after short network drops, and switches to TCP when a network blocks WebRTC.
- 🛡️ **Private by design**: encrypted connections, PIN only in memory, and your window is hidden from screenshots while you watch.

## Before you install

- **Windows** will show a SmartScreen warning because the installer isn't code-signed yet: click **More info → Run anyway**. The first time you host, allow ScreenShare through the firewall on **private** networks.
- **macOS**: the app isn't signed yet, so right-click it and choose **Open** the first time. Sharing needs the **Screen Recording** permission; system audio needs macOS 13 or newer. The macOS build hasn't been tested on a real Mac yet.

## 🇧🇷 Em português

Primeira versão do **ScreenShare**: compartilhe sua tela com as pessoas da sua rede, sem contas e sem nuvem.

- Baixe o instalador do **Windows** (`-x64.exe` na maioria dos PCs, `-arm64.exe` em notebooks ARM, ou o `ScreenShare-Setup-1.0.0.exe` que serve para os dois) ou o do **macOS** (`.dmg`, experimental). Todos na mesma sala precisam desta mesma versão.
- As salas aparecem sozinhas na rede local; em VPN, use **Connect by IP**. Salas públicas ou privadas com PIN.
- Qualquer pessoa pode compartilhar, várias ao mesmo tempo, com som do computador (e a opção **Leave out Discord** no Windows). Cada um escolhe o que assistir e em qual qualidade.
- Chat, fotos de perfil com recorte e controles para o anfitrião.
- O instalador ainda não é assinado: no Windows clique em **Mais informações → Executar assim mesmo**; no macOS clique com o botão direito no app → **Abrir**.
