Watching gets better: full screen now works like a video player, with nothing but the picture until you move the mouse, and Stats shows how busy the whole computer is. Playing with friends on other networks? There's a new step-by-step guide for [playing over ZeroTier](https://github.com/nsfxu/lan-screenshare/blob/main/docs/en-US/zerotier.md).

## Download

| System | File |
|---|---|
| **Windows 10/11**, most PCs | `ScreenShare-Setup-2.1.0-x64.exe` |
| **Windows 11 on ARM** (e.g. Snapdragon laptops) | `ScreenShare-Setup-2.1.0-arm64.exe` |
| **Mac with Apple Silicon** (M1 or newer), experimental | `ScreenShare-2.1.0-arm64.dmg` |
| **Mac with Intel**, experimental | `ScreenShare-2.1.0-x64.dmg` |

Works in the same rooms as 1.x and 2.x versions, so people can update when they like.

## What's new

#### Changed

- **No more blue border around the focused stream.**
- **Full screen works like a video player**: the stream fills the whole screen with no frame around it, and the strip and the controls float over its bottom instead of taking space. After a moment without moving the mouse everything but the picture hides, the name and the stats badges included; moving the mouse brings it back.

#### Added

- **Stats** also shows the whole computer's CPU and memory (games and other apps included), next to the app's, so you can tell when the computer itself is the bottleneck.

#### New guide

- **[Playing over ZeroTier](https://github.com/nsfxu/lan-screenshare/blob/main/docs/en-US/zerotier.md)**: friends on other networks join one ZeroTier network in a few minutes, and rooms then show up in the list as if you were all at home.

The installers aren't code-signed yet: on Windows click **More info → Run anyway**, on macOS right-click the app → **Open** the first time.

Full list of changes: [CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/nsfxu/lan-screenshare/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md)

## 🇧🇷 Em português

O 2.1.0 melhora a hora de assistir:

- **Tela cheia como num player de vídeo**: a transmissão ocupa a tela toda, sem moldura, e a faixa e os controles ficam por cima da parte de baixo dela. Depois de um tempo sem mexer o mouse, tudo some menos a imagem (nome e selos de estatística inclusive); mexer o mouse traz de volta.
- **Sem a borda azul** em volta da transmissão em destaque.
- **Stats** mostra também a CPU e a memória do computador inteiro (jogos inclusive), ao lado das do app.
- **Guia novo**: [jogando pelo ZeroTier](https://github.com/nsfxu/lan-screenshare/blob/main/docs/pt-BR/zerotier.md), para jogar com amigos em outras redes. As salas aparecem na lista como se todos estivessem em casa.

Funciona nas mesmas salas que as versões 1.x e 2.x. Os instaladores ainda não são assinados: no Windows clique em **Mais informações → Executar assim mesmo**; no macOS, clique com o botão direito no app → **Abrir** na primeira vez.
