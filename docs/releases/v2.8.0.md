See who's in a room before you join, reply to messages and mention people in chat, pin and rename your rooms, follow your computer's light or dark mode, and share windows from your other desktops on a Mac.

> 🇧🇷 Resumo em português no final.

## Download

| System | File |
|---|---|
| **Windows 10/11**, most PCs | `ScreenShare-Setup-2.8.0-x64.exe` |
| **Windows 11 on ARM** (e.g. Snapdragon laptops) | `ScreenShare-Setup-2.8.0-arm64.exe` |
| **Mac with Apple Silicon** (M1 or newer), experimental | `ScreenShare-2.8.0-arm64.dmg` |
| **Mac with Intel**, experimental | `ScreenShare-2.8.0-x64.dmg` |

Works in the same rooms as 1.x and 2.x versions, so people can update when they like. A few things need the room's host on 2.8.0: the pictures of who's inside (older hosts show the count) and replies (older apps show them as plain messages). On Windows, 2.2.0 and later update themselves: look for **Restart to update** in the title bar.

## What's new

#### Added

- **Match my computer** in **Settings → Appearance**: the app switches between a dark theme and a light theme of your choice as your computer changes between dark and light mode.
- **See who's inside a room before joining.** Public rooms in the room list and on the home screen show the pictures of the people inside (then *+N*), instead of just how many; hover over one for the name. Private rooms still show only the count, and so do rooms whose host hasn't updated.
- **Pin and rename your rooms.** Right-click a recent room (or use its **⋯**) to pin it to the top, where it stays even while it's offline, or give it a name of your own, like *Home PC*, that only you see. **Copy address** and **Forget this room** are there too.
- **Share windows from your other desktops on macOS.** The share picker only showed the windows on the desktop (Space) you were on. Windows on your other desktops are now listed too, after the others, with their app's icon instead of a preview, and you can share them without moving them.
- **Reply to messages and mention people in chat.** Point at a message and click **Reply**: yours shows it quoted on top. Type **@** to mention someone from a list; messages that mention you are highlighted, and the notification says so when the app is in the background.
- **Clickable links in chat.** Web addresses (`http://` or `https://`) in a message are links now and open in your browser.

#### Changed

- **More even spacing.** Gaps and margins across the app now follow one scale, so panels, menus and Settings line up the same way everywhere; a few spots get a little more room.
- **Offline rooms keep their host's picture.** A room you've been in that doesn't answer now shows, faded, its host's picture (or initials) from your last visit, instead of just a letter. It's kept on your computer only, and rooms remembered before this version get it the next time you join them.

#### Fixed

- **The screen stays on while you watch or share.** It could dim, sleep or lock in the middle of a stream when you didn't touch the mouse; now it only does that once you stop watching and sharing.
- **Sharing a window is smooth again.** Since 2.5.0, sharing a single window (not a whole screen) of detailed content could drop to about 10 frames a second on NVIDIA graphics cards, because the video was then encoded without the graphics card. Windows are now shared at full speed from the start, as before.

The installers aren't code-signed yet: on Windows click **More info → Run anyway**, on macOS open the app once, click **Done**, then **Open Anyway** in *System Settings → Privacy & Security* ([step by step](https://github.com/nsfxu/lan-screenshare/blob/main/docs/en-US/user-guide.md#opening-it-for-the-first-time-on-macos)).

## 🇧🇷 Em português

O 2.8.0 traz:

- **Veja quem está numa sala antes de entrar.** Salas públicas mostram as fotos de quem está dentro (depois *+N*); salas privadas continuam só com a contagem.
- **Responda e mencione no chat.** Aponte para uma mensagem e clique em **Responder**: a sua a mostra citada em cima. Digite **@** para mencionar alguém; mensagens que mencionam você ficam destacadas. Links da web agora são clicáveis.
- **Fixe e renomeie suas salas.** Clique com o botão direito numa sala recente (ou no **⋯**) para fixá-la no topo ou dar um nome só seu, como *PC de casa*.
- **Acompanhar o computador** em **Configurações → Aparência**: o app troca entre um tema escuro e um claro junto com o computador.
- **No Mac, janelas de outras mesas** (Spaces) aparecem no seletor para compartilhar.
- **Correções:** compartilhar uma janela está fluido de novo, a tela não apaga nem bloqueia enquanto você assiste ou transmite, e salas offline mantêm a foto de quem hospeda.

Funciona nas mesmas salas que as versões 1.x e 2.x. No Windows, quem tem o 2.2.0 ou mais novo recebe a atualização sozinho (**Reinicie para atualizar**). No macOS, na primeira vez feche o aviso e clique em **Abrir Mesmo Assim** em *Ajustes do Sistema → Privacidade e Segurança* ([passo a passo](https://github.com/nsfxu/lan-screenshare/blob/main/docs/pt-BR/user-guide.md#abrir-pela-primeira-vez-no-macos)). Os instaladores ainda não são assinados: no Windows clique em **Mais informações → Executar assim mesmo**.
